About

I am a final year Computer Science student at Sindh Madressatul Islam University in Karachi, focused on data analysis and the tools that turn raw information into decisions. My work centers on Python, SQL, and building interfaces that make data usable for people who are not analysts themselves. Over the past two years I have moved from classroom exercises into real internal systems, which has shaped how I think about accuracy, reliability, and what actually gets used once it ships.

During my internship at Fauji Fertilizer Company, I found the RM office still tracking assets through a manual spreadsheet with no real time visibility. I rebuilt it as a Python dashboard using Pandas for cleaning, Plotly for visuals, and Streamlit for the interface, adding login access, search filters, and automated status reports. That project taught me that most data problems are not really about the data. They are about the process around it, and a small, well placed tool can remove a lot of manual work.

I am drawn to this field because it rewards logical thinking and a comfort with numbers, both of which came naturally to me through mathematics. That same instinct has pulled me toward AI and machine learning projects alongside my core data work, from natural language tools to computer vision. My immediate goal is an entry level role in data analysis or database administration, with data science as the direction I am building toward as I gain more experience.
