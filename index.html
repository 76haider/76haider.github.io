01 / About

Who I am, briefly.

Final year CS student who learned data by breaking things, then fixing them properly.
SMIU Karachi
BSCS, Final Semester

I am in my final semester of a BS in Computer Science at Sindh Madressatul Islam University, Karachi. Most of what I know about data, I learned by solving real problems. The RM office at FFC was tracking assets on Excel with no real time visibility, so I rebuilt it as a Python dashboard with Pandas, Plotly, and Streamlit.

I like problems with a clear right answer hiding in messy data, which is why the math and logic side of this field pulls me in, not just the tools. Lately that has meant pushing past dashboards into NLP and computer vision: an ambiguity detector for newspaper text, a facial recognition attendance system, a semantic analyzer for long documents.

I am graduating into an entry level search. Data Analyst is my focus right now, with Database Administrator work as a strong parallel track given how much of my project work is database backed. Down the line I am aiming toward Data Science, but that is a goal, not a claim I am making today.
