I am a final year Computer Science student at Sindh Madressatul Islam University, Karachi. My entry into data came from seeing how real systems work when humans get out of the way of repetitive tasks. Whether automating tracking or structuring records, I focus on building reliable software and analytics that save time.

I focus heavily on the logic and structured database side of computer science.
