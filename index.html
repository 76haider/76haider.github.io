Who I am, briefly.

A short introduction to who I am, what I build, and where I am headed.
Muhammad Haider
Data Analyst
SMIU Karachi (BSCS)

Karachi, Pakistan

I am in my final semester of a BS in Computer Science at Sindh Madressatul Islam University, Karachi, focused on turning raw data into dashboards and analysis that people actually use. Most of what I know about data, I learned by solving real problems. The RM office at FFC was tracking assets on Excel with no real time visibility, so I rebuilt it as a Python dashboard with Pandas, Plotly, and Streamlit.

My core stack is Python, SQL, and interactive dashboards. I use these to clean messy data, structure it into something meaningful, and deliver it in a form a non technical team can read at a glance. What pulls me in is the maths and logic side of this field, not just the tools. Give me a problem with a right answer hiding somewhere in the mess, and that is where I do my best work.

I care about doing things properly, not just quickly. I would rather build one dashboard a team actually depends on than ten that look good in a demo. If you are looking for someone who will show up, ask the boring questions, and keep learning until the problem is really solved, that is the kind of work I want to be doing.
