01 / About

Who I am, briefly.

Final-year CS student who learned data by breaking things, then fixing them properly.

SMIU Karachi
BSCS · Final semester

I'm in my final semester of a BS in Computer Science at Sindh Madressatul Islam University, Karachi. Most of what I know about data, I learned by solving real problems — the RM office at FFC was tracking assets on Excel with no real-time visibility, so I rebuilt it as a Python dashboard with Pandas, Plotly, and Streamlit.

I like problems with a clear right answer hiding in messy data — which is why the math and logic side of this field pulls me in, not just the tools. Lately that's meant pushing past dashboards into NLP and computer vision: an ambiguity detector for newspaper text, a facial-recognition attendance system, a semantic analyzer for long documents.

I'm graduating into an entry-level search — Data Analyst roles first, with Database Administrator work as a strong parallel track given how much of my project work is database-backed. Data Science is the direction, not the day-one claim.
